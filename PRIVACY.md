# Privacy policy — Storyteller Mobile

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

*Versión en español: [PRIVACY.es.md](PRIVACY.es.md)*

## 1. Data controller and scope

The Storyteller Mobile application (the "App") is published on Google Play by [REDACTED], with address at [REDACTED] (the "Provider"), under the developer name javimentallab. The Provider is the controller of the personal data referred to in sections 3 and 7, and the User may contact the Provider at [REDACTED]. The App has no user accounts, advertising, analytics tools, crash reporting systems or server of its own.

## 2. Data transmitted outside the device

The App transmits only the data required for each operation, and exclusively to the service that the User has set up for that operation in the *Models* section, usually an artificial intelligence service:

- **Narration:** the text of the book, sentence by sentence, and, where applicable, the voice recording uploaded by the User.
- **Transcription:** the audio of the imported audiobook, in two-minute segments, and, if the User enables the check of narrated sentences, the audio of those sentences as well.
- **Translation:** the text of the document.

Each request includes the access key provided by the User for the relevant service. The provider of each service can access the data sent to it, under its own terms, so the User should choose services the User trusts. Before narrating, the App informs the User of the amount of text to be sent and of its recipient; before translating, it informs the User of the recipient. The Provider has no access to these data and takes no part in their processing, which is the responsibility of the providers of the services chosen by the User.

If the address of a service is outside the User's home network and does not use HTTPS, the App warns that the text would be transmitted unencrypted and does not send the access key to that address.

## 3. Reporting content

The *Report content* function opens a Google form within the App. The information the User enters in it reaches the Provider through Google, under Google's terms. The App adds no other data: neither the books, nor the access keys, nor any other information.

- **Purpose:** to handle the report and, where appropriate, to take action against misuse of the App.
- **Legal basis:** the Provider's legitimate interest in handling reports and preventing misuse of the App (Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation).
- **Retention period:** as long as necessary to handle the report. It is then deleted, unless it must be kept, duly blocked, for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims during the applicable limitation periods.
- **Recipients:** Google, as the provider of the form service, and no one else, except where required by law.

## 4. Data retention

The retention periods for reports and for purchase data are set out in sections 3 and 7. Retention of data by the services chosen by the User is governed by their own terms.

On the device, the books, their audio and subtitles, translations, settings and pronunciation dictionaries remain in the App's own folders until the User deletes them or uninstalls the App. Access keys are stored encrypted with the Android Keystore. Uninstalling the App deletes all of this data, except for files the User has saved elsewhere with the *Save as* function.

## 5. Backups

Android cloud backup is disabled for this App. On some devices, when moving to a new device, the App's data may be copied directly from one device to the other. Access keys are not preserved in that process, since the key that encrypts them never leaves the original device, so the User will have to enter them again.

## 6. Permissions

From Android 13 onwards, the App asks the User for the notifications permission, which it uses to report the progress of each operation and its completion. The other permissions used by the App are granted when it is installed and are listed on its Google Play page.

## 7. Purchase data

The App is purchased through Google Play, which handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The Provider does not receive the User's payment details. For each order, Google provides the Provider with limited data, such as the order number, the date, the amount and the buyer's country.

As required by Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (General Data Protection Regulation), the following information is provided:

- **Purpose:** to manage orders and refunds and to comply with the Provider's accounting and tax obligations.
- **Legal basis:** the performance of the purchase contract (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation) and compliance with the Provider's legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).
- **Retention period:** the period required by tax law; as a general rule, four years (Article 66 of the Spanish General Taxation Act). After that period, the data are deleted.
- **Recipients:** the data are not disclosed to third parties, except where required by law.
